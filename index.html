FAQ

Good to know.

How much notice do you need?
I prefer about 2 weeks' notice so I can plan your cake properly. If your date is sooner, still message me. I can often fit shorter notice orders in.
Do you only make buttercream cakes?
Yes. I specialise in buttercream, so every cake is finished in smooth, hand-piped buttercream rather than fondant.
What sizes do you offer?
I make 4 inch, 6 inch and 8 inch cakes, plus boxes of cupcakes. Not sure which size you need? Try the guest slider above, or ask me.
Can I bring my own toppers or decorations?
Yes. If you're on a budget, you can buy your own toppers and decorations and I'll put them on your cake for you. Just let me know when you order.
Is there a minimum order for cupcakes?
Yes. Cupcakes come in boxes of 12, which is the minimum order. A box starts from £35.
Do you deliver?
Yes. You can collect your cake, or I can arrange delivery through an external delivery company. Delivery isn't included in the cake price, and we'll talk through the details and cost by Instagram DM or email.
Can I order through this website?
Not directly. This site is a look book of my work. To order, send me a DM on Instagram or fill in my order form, and I'll take it from there.
How do I secure my date?
Once we've agreed your design, I'll send you an invoice. Your date is booked once your 50% deposit is received.
